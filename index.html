Do owners ask for lean?

Owners are the organizations that can require lean on their projects. The largest public owner in the United States is the federal government, whose construction solicitations are public with their full text. This view reads what those solicitations ask for, shows the only notices that name a lean method, how the awards of the panel firms are written, and which private and public owners have joined the Lean Construction Institute.
